# Controls & Interaction

## Mouse Controls
- **Left Click & Drag**: Rotate camera around the focus point.
- **Right Click & Drag**: Pan the camera focus point (moves the camera left/right/up/down).
- **Scroll Wheel**: Zoom in and out.
- **Left Click (Bottom Bar)**: Select a planet to focus on its orbit and display its data.

## Keyboard Controls
### Movement
- **W / Up Arrow**: Zoom In
- **S / Down Arrow**: Zoom Out
- **A / Left Arrow**: Rotate camera Left
- **D / Right Arrow**: Rotate camera Right
- **Q / E**: Tilt camera Up / Down

### Simulation State
- **Spacebar**: Play / Pause simulation.
- **+ / =**: Increase simulation speed.
- **- / _**: Decrease simulation speed.
- **R**: Reset camera to default overview.

### Visual Toggles
- **O**: Toggle orbit trails (lines tracing the planets' paths).
- **V**: Toggle wireframe mode (renders planets as lines instead of solid polygons).
- **F**: Toggle Fullscreen mode.

### View Presets
- **1**: Solar System Overview (Default)
- **2**: Top View
- **3**: Side View
- **4**: Front View
- **5**: Focus on the currently selected planet.
- **ESC**: Exit application.
